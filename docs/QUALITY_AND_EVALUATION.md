# Quality and Evaluation

## Purpose
Quality review helps users identify likely defects and decide what to revise. It is advisory, not a certification system.

## Image review dimensions
- Brief alignment
- Subject identity and attribute consistency
- Composition and visual hierarchy
- Lighting coherence
- Perspective and optical plausibility
- Material and texture fidelity
- Color and atmosphere
- Anatomy or structural defects where applicable
- Text and logo fidelity when required
- Output dimensions and format

## Video review dimensions
- Brief and shot plan alignment
- Temporal consistency
- Subject and object continuity
- Motion plausibility
- Camera movement coherence
- Scene and lighting continuity
- Transition quality
- Flicker, warping, morphing, and frame defects
- Duration and output specification compliance

## Finding structure
Each finding should include severity, affected area or timestamp, concise explanation, confidence, and suggested correction. Separate observed evidence from inferred causes.

## Scoring
A score may summarize review results only if its dimensions and weighting are visible. Never imply that a score is objective truth. Show confidence and known blind spots.

## Review limitations
State whether the review used the full asset, selected frames, thumbnails, metadata, or model generated descriptions. Do not claim full frame inspection if only sampled frames were checked.

## Revision logic
Translate a finding into the smallest relevant change. Preserve approved decisions that are not implicated. Explain when the provider may regenerate the entire asset and cannot guarantee local edits.

## Evaluation set
Create a small manually reviewed test set covering common image and video briefs, references, complex lighting, motion, continuity, text fidelity, and failure states. Track regressions across engine and provider changes.
